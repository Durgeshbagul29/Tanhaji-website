About The Game

The First RPG on History of Maratha Warriors

Tanhaji – The Lion Maratha Warrior is a free 3D role-playing game created as a tribute to the legendary warrior Tanhaji Malusare and dedicated to the great Maratha king, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. As the first RPG based on Maratha warriors and the first 3D game in Indian Maratha history, it honors the bravery of countless soldiers who laid down their lives to build the Maratha Empire. The game immerses players in the rich legacy of Tanhaji, offering a unique opportunity to relive the epic mission to capture Kondhana Fort.

In this action-packed adventure, players must fight their way through Mughal forces using swords and strategic combat inspired by Shivaji Maharaj’s guerrilla warfare tactics (Ganimi Kawa). The open-world environment allows for dynamic gameplay, including battles with wild animals like wolves and tigers. As players progress through 12 challenging levels, they rise in rank from Mavala to Hetkari, with each stage offering new enemies and experiences. The game’s realistic music and intelligent horse companion—summoned by a whistle—add depth and excitement to the journey.

The Fort Kondhana

As Veer Tanhaji, your journey begins with a fierce confrontation against a tiger guarding the entrance to Kondhana Fort—a symbolic test of courage before the real battle begins. The fort is heavily fortified by Mughal soldiers, each wave more relentless than the last. Among them stand two formidable foes: the Paheredar, a vigilant sentinel with unmatched agility, and the Killedar, a brutal warlord commanding the fort’s defenses. You must use your swordsmanship, strategic thinking, and mastery of guerrilla warfare to overcome these enemies and reclaim the fort for the Maratha Empire.

The path to victory is steep—literally. You’ll scale the rugged cliffs of Kondhana, navigating treacherous terrain and ambushes as you ascend toward the final showdown. Along the way, collect potions to restore your strength and sharpen your reflexes. With the blessings of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Bhawani Maata, your spirit remains unbreakable. Every clash echoes the cry of “Har Har Mahadev,” reminding you that this mission is not just a battle—it’s a legacy. The fate of the fort, and the pride of the Maratha warriors, rests in your hands.
