About The Game

Experience the epic tale of bravery and valor in this groundbreaking Indian RPG

Tanhaji: The Lion Maratha Warrior

India's first RPG based on the legendary Maratha warrior, Tanhaji Malusare. This groundbreaking game brings to life the heroic tale of the brave warrior who fought valiantly to recapture the Kondhana Fort from the Mughals in the 17th century.

Developed by AbracaDabra Software Solutions Pvt. Ltd., this game is a tribute to Indian history and showcases the rich cultural heritage of the Maratha Empire. Experience breathtaking battles, strategic gameplay, and an immersive storyline that will keep you engaged for hours.

System Requirements

Minimum and recommended system specifications for Tanhaji Steam Edition

Minimum Requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i5-4460 or AMD FX-6300
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760 or AMD Radeon R7 260x
  • DirectX: Version 11
  • Storage: 15 GB available space

Recommended Requirements

  • OS: Windows 10/11 64-bit
  • Processor: Intel Core i7-4770 or AMD Ryzen 5 1600
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • Graphics: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1060 or AMD Radeon RX 580
  • DirectX: Version 12
  • Storage: 15 GB available space (SSD recommended)
